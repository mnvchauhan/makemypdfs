The Story Behind the Code

It started with a simple problem. We were working on a college project and needed to merge some PDF files quickly. Every tool we found online was either painfully slow, bombarded us with intrusive ads, or hit us with a paywall asking for a $15/month subscription just to click a button.

That frustration sparked an idea in Delhi. Why not build the exact tool we wished existed? Something clean, lightning-fast, and completely 100% free.

What began as a late-night coding experiment to solve our own problem has rapidly grown into a premium workspace processing thousands of files securely for professionals worldwide. We believe essential utility tools should be accessible to everyone.
