The story behind
MakeMyPDFs.

Hi, I'm Manav Chauhan, a full-stack developer from Delhi. I was working on a project and needed to quickly merge some PDFs. Every tool I found was either painfully slow, bombarded me with ads, or asked for a $15/month subscription just to click a button.

That frustration sparked an idea. I decided to build the exact tool I wished existed: clean, lightning-fast, and 100% free. What started as a late-night coding experiment is now processing thousands of files securely for professionals worldwide.
